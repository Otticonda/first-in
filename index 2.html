Find the wallets that keep getting in early.

Paste tokens that ran. First In reads each one's launch history on-chain, pulls the first buyers with their entry and profit, and ranks the wallets that show up early across several of them. Watch the good ones and see what they buy next.

Connect a free RPC

  1. Sign up at dashboard.helius.dev. The free plan is $0 and needs no card.
  2. Copy your API key from the dashboard.
  3. Paste it below. It stays in this browser only.

Any Solana RPC URL that allows browser requests works too. The free Helius plan gives 1M credits a month; a scan uses roughly 300–2,000 including profit data.

Works best on pump.fun launches. Other tokens are read from the mint's own history, which can be slow for very active coins.
